## Math sniff test
~$50 → $5,000 ≈ **100× in 48 hours**. Sustained returns at that rate dwarf top quant funds’ annual numbers and conflict with Polymarket CLOB min sizes, spreads, and fees for tiny bankrolls. Treat as fiction until on-chain wallet evidence.

## Firm relevance (this repo)
- Do **not** fund Polymarket/trading experiments from firm cash without explicit owner authorization + spend cap.  
- Do **not** pitch customers “AI agent turns $50 into $5k.”  
- Catalog stays website / scoped automation packages with evidence-backed outcomes.  
- If owner wants a product angle later: honest deliverable = research report or fail-closed sim — never promised multiples.
