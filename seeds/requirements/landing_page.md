# Requirements — Conversion landing page

Honest scope from the firm offer catalog (`landing_page`). Not a mock brief.

## Buyer problem
No focused page that states one offer and captures a lead or booking.

## In scope
- One responsive landing page (mobile + desktop)
- Brand/headline/CTA aligned to an agreed written brief
- Lead CTA or form placeholder wired to an owner-provided endpoint (email, Formspree, Calendly embed, etc.)
- Preview URL + source handoff

## Out of scope
- Ongoing SEO campaign management
- Paid ads management
- Full brand identity system
- Multi-page CMS rebuilds (Mindbody full sites, online shops, member video libraries)

## Price and timebox
- USD 1,800–5,000 depending on copy/assets readiness
- Typical delivery: 7 days after brief + assets received

## Acceptance
- Page loads on mobile and desktop
- Brand, headline, and CTA match the agreed brief
- Owner accepts the preview in writing before handoff

## What we need from you to start
1. One sentence offer (e.g. “$99 unlimited first month”)
2. Logo + 2–4 photos or permission to use placeholders
3. Destination for leads (email inbox, CRM webhook, or booking link)
4. Written approval to proceed after quote
